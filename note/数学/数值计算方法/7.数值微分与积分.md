# 数值微分与积分

## 1. 数值微分

### 1.1. 差商型求导公式

(1) 向前差商公式

$$
f'(x) = \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$

余项： $R(x)=-\frac{f''(x+\theta h)}{2}h, theta\in(0, 1)$

(2) 向后差商公式

$$
f'(x) = \frac{f(x)-f(x-h)}{h}
$$

余项： $R(x)=\frac{f''(x-\theta h)}{2}h, theta\in(0, 1)$

(3) 中心差商公式

$$
f'(x) = \frac{f(x+h)-f(x-h)}{2h}
$$

余项： $R(x)=-\frac{f^{(3)}(x+\theta_{1} h)+f^{(3)}(x-\theta_{2} h}{12}h^{2}, theta_{0}, \theta_{1}\in(0, 1)$


### 1.2. 插值型求导公式

(1) 两点公式

过节点 $x_{0}, x_{1}=x_{0}+h$ 的 Lagrange 插值多项式为

$$
L_{1}(x) = \frac{x-x_{1}}{-h}f(x_{0})+\frac{x-x_{0}}{h}f(x_{1})
$$

故有

$$
f'(x_{0})\approx L_{1}(x_{0})=\frac{f(x_{1})-f(x_{0})}{h}
$$

$$
f'(x_{1})\approx L_{1}(x_{1})=\frac{f(x_{1})-f(x_{0})}{h}
$$

截断误差：

$$
R_{1}'(x_{0})=-\frac{h}{2}f''(\xi_{0})
$$

$$
R_{1}'(x_{1})=\frac{h}{2}f''(\xi_{1})
$$

其中 $\xi_{0}， \xi_{1} \in (x_{0}, x_{1})$


(2) 三点公式

过节点 $x_{i}=x_{0}+ih, i=0, 1, 2$, $f(x)$ 的 Lagrange 插值多项式为

$$
L_{2}(x) = \frac{(x-x_{1}(x-x_{2})}{2h^{2}}f(x_{0}) + \frac{(x-x_{0})(x-x_{2})}{-h^{2}}f(x_{1}) + \frac{(x-x_{0})(x-x_{1})}{2h^{2}}f(x_{2})
$$

求导得

$$
L_{2}'(x) = \frac{2x-x_{1}-x_{2}}{2h^{2}}f(x_{0}) - \frac{2x-x_{0}-x_{2}}{h^{2}}f(x_{1}) + \frac{2x-x_{0}-x_{1}}{2h^{2}}f(x_{2})
$$

可得三点公式

$$
f'(x_{0})\approx \frac{1}{2h}[-3f(x_{0})+4f(x_{1}-f(x_{2})]
$$

$$
f'(x_{1})\approx \frac{1}{2h}[-f(x_{0})+f(x_{2})]
$$

$$
f'(x_{2})\approx \frac{1}{2h}[f(x_{0})-4f(x_{1}+3f(x_{2})]
$$

截断误差：

$$
R_{2}'(x_{0}) = \frac{h^{2}}{3}f^{(3)}(\xi_{0})
$$

$$
R_{2}'(x_{1}) = -\frac{h^{2}}{6}f^{(3)}(\xi_{1})
$$

$$
R_{2}'(x_{2}) = \frac{h^{2}}{3}f^{(3)}(\xi_{2})
$$

求二阶导数的三点公式：

$$
f''(x_{i}) \approx L_{2}''(x_{i}) = \frac{1}{h^{2}}[f(x_{0}) - 2f(x_{1} +f(x_{2}))]
$$
